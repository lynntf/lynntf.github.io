# Thomas F. Lynn

## Welcome

I am Senior Professional Staff at the Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Argonne National Laboratory. I received my Ph.D. from Northwestern University studying Applied Mathematics.

## Research

I develop machine learning and optimization methods for scientific problems; in particular, methods that learn *what to optimize*: objective functions grounded in physics and data, rather than only learning predictors. This includes machine learning for gamma-ray tracking ([GRETO](https://github.com/lynntf/GRETO)), optimal experimental design, and physics-informed models.

In my Ph.D., I studied mixing due to *cutting-and-shuffling* as it applies to systems of solid granular materials.

Argonne [featured our gamma-ray tracking work for GRETA](https://www.anl.gov/article/opening-a-new-eye-on-the-atomic-nucleus) in a recent press release.
